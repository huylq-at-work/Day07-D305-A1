# Registrar FAQs

1. Academic Programs & Registration 

What does the Registrar’s Office do? 

 It manages student records, course registration, academic calendars, graduation review, and official documents. 

What is considered full-time enrollment? 

 Students must register for at least 80% of the standard annual credit load (usually around 30 credits per year). 

Can I switch majors or colleges? 

 Yes, students may request to change programs or colleges, subject to review and approval. 

How do I apply for a double degree? 

Submit a request to Registrar. The process includes credit equivalency review, faculty approval, and Provost’s confirmation. 

When are classes scheduled at VinUni? 

Classes are typically held between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM on weekdays. Class periods are either 50 or 75 minutes. 

When will I receive my study plan for the year? 

Colleges typically publish the updated study plan for each cohort before the start of the academic year. Contact your College Office for access. 

Where can I find the academic catalog or program structure? 

 You should consult your College for the official academic catalog, which outlines your study plan, program requirements, and course mapping by cohort. 

Where can I find tuition fee information? 

 Tuition and fee details are provided by the Finance Office. For academic planning, contact the Registrar if course load affects tuition. 

2. Credit & Academic Standing 

Can I transfer credits? 

Yes, if the course content and learning outcomes are deemed equivalent. 

How is academic standing calculated? 

Based on GPA and progression status, as outlined in the academic regulations. 

How many credits can I register for per semester? 

Typically, 15 credits per semester. Approval is needed for overloads. 

3. Academic Integrity 

What happens if I violate academic integrity? 

Violations are classified into Tiers 1–4 with increasing penalties, from verbal warning to suspension or dismissal. 

What is Tier 1 violation? 

Minor or unintentional offenses (e.g., poor citation) – verbal warning only. 

What is Tier 2 violation? 

Intentional or repeated misconduct – may result in zero grade for assignment or exam. 

What is Tier 3 violation? 

Serious or repeated offenses – may result in course failure or suspension. 

What is Tier 4 violation? 

Severe misconduct (e.g., impersonation, cheating in exams) may lead to dismissal or revocation of a degree. 

Who handles academic integrity cases? 

Instructor (Tier 1-2) 

Program Director, Dean, Registrar (Tier 3) 

Academic Integrity Council (Tier 4) 

Are academic integrity violations recorded? 

Yes, from Tier 2 and above, the cases are recorded in the Registrar’s system. 

4. Graduation & Degree Conferral 

How does the graduation process work? 

Includes student review, department and university-level evaluation, and final approval by the Provost.

What is the graduation timeline? 

From March to August: student review, transcript check, program board evaluation, decision issuance, and certificate printing. 

Can I receive a provisional certificate? 

Yes, after graduation is approved, a provisional certificate may be issued upon request. 

How long are graduation records stored? 

Registrar retains all graduation documents for the legally required duration. 

5. Master’s Programs 

What are the regulations for Master’s programs? 

Regulations differ for research and application-oriented tracks, with requirements for philosophy, English proficiency, and thesis/project. 

When will I receive my Master’s degree? 

Within one month after the official decision on graduation is released.

How are Master’s records stored? 

Includes transcripts, thesis, defense reports, and are retained for at least 5 years. 

6. Contact & Support 

Who can I contact for registration support? 

Email [email protected] or call ext. 9004 during working hours. 

What is the Registrar’s role in First-Year Advising? 

The Registrar supports advising logistics and collaborates with Student Affairs to implement the First-Year Faculty Advising Program. 

Does VinUni follow international standards? 

Yes. VinUni is a member of AACRAO, adhering to global academic administration practices.

Can I change my Faculty Advisor? 

Yes, by submitting a request to Student Affairs Management. 

7. General Policies 

Who is responsible for enforcing academic regulations? 

Colleges, Registrar, and relevant offices. The Provost may amend policies when needed.

What is the official language of instruction? 

English is the primary medium. Some bilingual delivery may apply.

Can academic policies change? 

Yes, academic policies are subject to updates based on MOET or internal decisions. Registrar is responsible for communicating changes.
